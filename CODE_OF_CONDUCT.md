# Code of Conduct

BrainLearn contributors, maintainers, reviewers, and participants are expected
to make project spaces respectful and useful for people with different
backgrounds and levels of experience. Scientific disagreement is welcome when
it addresses the evidence and assumptions without attacking the person.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and specific in issues, reviews, meetings, and
  other project spaces.
- Explain technical and scientific criticism with evidence, and make room for
  questions and corrections.
- Respect privacy, consent, and data-use restrictions. Do not pressure anyone
  to disclose research data, personal information, or unpublished results.
- Credit the work of contributors and distinguish implementation evidence from
  scientific approval.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or repeated personal
  attacks.
- Unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, or disclosure of private communications
  or personal information without consent.
- Misrepresenting another person's work, review, scientific endorsement, or
  contribution.
- Sharing identifiable, restricted, or confidential research material without
  authorization.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not post sensitive incident details in a public issue or pull request.
Contact the project maintainer privately using a contact method listed on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/mohammedsadeghi). The profile
currently does not publish an email address, and BrainLearn has no independent
conduct committee; a dedicated confidential reporting contact must be
established before public-beta recruitment. If there is no private route
available to you, open an issue titled **“Private conduct contact requested”**
without names or incident details so the maintainer can arrange one.

The maintainer will review reports, seek enough information to understand what
happened, and take proportionate action. This may include a warning, removal of
content, restrictions on participation, or closing a contribution. Reports
will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible; no response time or
independent appeal process is promised at this stage. There is currently no
independent enforcement contact for reports involving the maintainer. Do not
send sensitive details to that person in such a case; this gap must be resolved
before external beta participants are invited.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, review, project
meetings, and other spaces used for BrainLearn work. The
[Governance](GOVERNANCE.md) page describes the current project roles and
decision process.
